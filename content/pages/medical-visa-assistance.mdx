## Documents typically required

- Valid passport with sufficient validity
- Passport-size photographs
- Hospital appointment or invitation letter
- Recent medical reports and doctor’s referral
- Completed visa application (embassy or e-visa where eligible)

## How we help

After your case is accepted by a partner hospital, we coordinate the invitation letter required for medical visa and medical attendant visa applications. Requirements vary by nationality — we provide country-specific guidance.
