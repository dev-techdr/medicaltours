Founded to make medical value travel simple and trustworthy, Techdr Medical Tourism partners with accredited hospitals in Hyderabad, Delhi NCR, Chennai, Mumbai, Bangalore, and other major cities.

We are a 30+ person care coordination team led by co-founders Raviteja Pendari and Abhista Gorityala (MBA, Healthcare Management) — not a hospital, and we do not pressure patients into procedures. We work with formal MOUs, clear written estimates, and a single point of contact from first inquiry through post-treatment follow-up. Together we have helped thousands of international patients and families plan treatment in India with less stress and clearer costs.

## Our mission

To connect international patients with the right Indian specialists and hospitals — safely, affordably, and without the confusion that often surrounds medical tourism.
