title: "Why India for Medical Treatment?"
description: "International patients choose India for accredited hospitals, specialist expertise, and 50–80% cost savings versus the US or UK."
---

## Significant cost savings

Heart surgery, joint replacement, cancer care, and dental work often cost a fraction of Western prices — even after flights and hotels.

## Accredited hospitals

JCI and NABH accredited centers with modern ICUs, imaging, and surgical technology serve hundreds of thousands of international patients each year.

## Specialist expertise

High case volumes mean surgeons perform procedures daily, not occasionally — especially in cardiac, orthopedic, oncology, and transplant care.

## Complete patient ecosystem

Medical visas, attendant visas, interpreters, and recovery hotels are well established across major Indian cities.
