Logistics are often the most stressful part of medical travel. We coordinate arrival transfers, hospital admission timing, and accommodation for the patient and family before, during, and after treatment.

- Airport pickup and drop-off in major cities
- Hotels and guest houses near hospitals
- Attendant stay planning during admission
- Local SIM and orientation support on request
