## About Pushpagiri Vitreo Retina Institute

Pushpagiri Vitreo Retina Institute (PVRI) is a TechdrHealth partner eye hospital in West Marredpally, Hyderabad (Secunderabad). The centre is known for vitreo-retinal surgery alongside cataract, cornea, and glaucoma care — with written package estimates, admission coordination, and discharge support.

## International & domestic patient services

TechdrHealth coordinates scan review, treatment packages, admission dates, and travel logistics for patients treated at PVRI Hyderabad.
