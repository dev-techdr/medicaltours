## About Star Hospitals

Star Hospitals is a TechdrHealth partner centre in Banjara Hills, Hyderabad, for planned surgery and multi-specialty care. The campus is known for cardiac sciences, orthopaedics, neurosciences, and critical care — with written package estimates, admission coordination, and discharge support.
