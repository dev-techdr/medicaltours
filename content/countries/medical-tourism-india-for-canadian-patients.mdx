Joint replacement, cardiac surgery, cancer care, IVF, dental implants, spine, and selected cosmetic pathways are the most common Canadian enquiries. An India package is evaluated against wait time at home, clinical fit at an accredited centre, and all-in USD pricing with a CAD budgeting reference.
