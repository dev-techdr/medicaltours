## Medical treatment in India for Rwandan patients

Patients from Kigali and across Rwanda travel to India for complex specialty care that may require referral abroad. TechdrHealth matches Rwandan patients with accredited hospitals and clarifies package inclusions before you fly.

Kigali flights typically connect through Nairobi, Dubai, or Addis Ababa into Indian metros. We align hospital admission dates with visa validity and attendant travel so families are not caught between flight tickets and bed availability.

## Popular care pathways for Rwanda

Oncology, cardiac surgery, joint replacement, and fertility care are among the most requested pathways. We compare doctors and hospitals against your reports so families can choose clinical fit — not a fixed referral list.

Ask specifically about what investigations are included, how many hospital nights are covered, and what triggers additional billing before you commit to non-refundable fares.
