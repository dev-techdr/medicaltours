## Medical treatment in India for American patients

Uninsured, underinsured, and self-pay Americans travel to India for cardiac, orthopedic, dental, bariatric, cancer, and spine care at JCI-accredited hospitals. TechdrHealth focuses on transparent package comparisons and practical travel planning — not a single-hospital sales pitch.

Many US patients arrive after collecting cash-pay quotes at home that feel unreachable. An India package often consolidates surgeon, theatre, hospital stay, and basic logistics into a clearer range quoted in USD.

## Evaluating value without compromising standards

Savings matter only when hospitals and surgeons are verifiable. We shortlist accredited partners, explain what the package includes and excludes, and outline stay length before you book flights. Ask specifically about implants, ICU days, attendant lodging, and what triggers additional billing.

India does not replace US emergency care. It is a planned pathway for elective or semi-elective specialty treatment when self-pay economics and specialist volume favour travelling.

## Insurance, self-pay, and documentation for US follow-up

Most international packages are self-pay. If you hold US insurance, confirm out-of-network or reimbursement rules with your carrier before travel — TechdrHealth can help assemble invoices and clinical records for claims teams, but coverage decisions remain with the insurer.

Before you leave India, collect operative notes, implant documentation, and imaging on a drive or secure transfer so your US physician can continue care without delay.

## Continuity of care with your home doctor

American families often worry that travel will break follow-up. We treat discharge documentation as part of the plan: operative notes, implant brands and lot information where relevant, imaging, and medication lists in formats your US clinician can use.
