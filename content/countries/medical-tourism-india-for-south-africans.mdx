## Medical treatment in India for South African patients

Patients from Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, and Pretoria travel to India when specialty depth, wait times, or private costs make overseas care a practical option. TechdrHealth matches South African patients with accredited hospitals and clarifies package inclusions before you fly.

Long-haul flights are part of the calculus. Families typically weigh airfare and attendant lodging against the gap between local private quotes and an India package at a JCI or NABH centre — especially for oncology, joints, cardiac surgery, and IVF.

## Popular care pathways for South Africa

Oncology, joint replacement, cardiac surgery, and IVF are among the most requested pathways. We compare doctors and hospitals against your reports so families can choose based on clinical fit, theatre volume, and recovery infrastructure.

Ask specifically about implant brands, chemotherapy protocols, IVF lab standards, and what happens if complications extend hospital stay. Clear answers before travel matter more than a headline price.

## Flights, visa, and rand planning

Common routings connect Johannesburg or Cape Town to Indian metros via Dubai or other hubs. After remote review, a hospital invitation letter supports the medical visa application; attendants can usually travel with the patient.

Packages are quoted in USD with an approximate ZAR planning reference. Confirm FX and transfer methods with the hospital finance team before departure, and keep a buffer for attendant costs and extended recovery.

We arrange second opinions, visa invitation letters, airport pickup, accommodation near the hospital, and recovery planning for South African patients and attendants. Discharge notes and imaging are prepared so your clinician at home can continue care after you return.
