## Medical treatment in India for UAE residents

Residents of Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and other emirates use India for specialty and elective care with convenient flight access — often a two- to four-hour hop to major Indian metros. TechdrHealth arranges hospital matching, written package estimates, and travel logistics end to end so you are not comparing hospitals through unstructured WhatsApp forwards alone.

Because many UAE residents hold different passport nationalities, visa category and invitation-letter requirements vary. We confirm the correct pathway for your nationality and planned length of stay before you pay hospital deposits.

## Common care pathways from the UAE

IVF and fertility treatment, oncology (surgery and systemic therapy), knee and hip replacement, cardiac procedures, and selected cosmetic or dental pathways are among the most requested reasons UAE families travel. Packages are typically quoted in USD with an approximate AED reference for planning.

Families often ask whether to stay in India for an entire chemo course or return between cycles. We map flight frequency, visa validity, and lodging near the treating centre so the calendar is realistic — not optimistic marketing.

## Language, attendants, and recovery stay

Arabic and English support, nearby hotels, and clear cost explanations help families complete treatment with less administrative friction. Attendants are common for major surgery; we help plan hotel blocks near campus and airport pickup for both patient and family.

After discharge, operative notes and imaging are shared in formats your home clinician can use if you continue follow-up in the UAE or elsewhere.
