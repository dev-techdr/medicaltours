## Medical treatment in India for Iraqi patients

Iraq-to-India medical travel is well established for tertiary specialties when local specialist queues are long or private quotes are high. TechdrHealth focuses on clear hospital shortlists, Arabic or English coordination, and logistics that reduce stress for attendants travelling from Baghdad, Basra, Erbil, and other cities.

Families often arrive after years of managing chronic cardiac or oncologic disease. Remote review of echo, angiography, MRI, or pathology helps confirm whether surgery, stenting, chemo, or transplant evaluation is the right next step before visas and flights are booked.

## Clinical focus for Iraqi families

Cardiac surgery, cancer protocols, kidney transplant evaluation, and spine care are among the most requested pathways. Packages and stay length are explained in writing before you commit to travel — including what happens if ICU days extend or additional investigations are needed.

For transplant pathways, donor documentation and legal compatibility must be confirmed early. Do not pay large deposits until the hospital transplant team accepts the case for evaluation.

## Flights, visa, and recovery planning

Common routes connect through regional hubs into Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, or Chennai. After invitation letters are issued, attendants can usually apply alongside the patient. Budget for hotel nights near campus, local transport, and a buffer if recovery is slower than expected.
