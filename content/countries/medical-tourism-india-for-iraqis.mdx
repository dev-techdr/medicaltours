Accredited centres combine high procedure volumes with international patient desks that work in English and arrange Arabic interpreters for consent and ward communication. ## Flights, visa, and recovery planning

Common routes connect Baghdad, Erbil, and Basra through regional hubs into Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, or Chennai. After invitation letters are issued, attendants can usually apply alongside the patient. Budget for hotel nights near campus, local transport, and a buffer if recovery is slower than expected.

Pack digital and printed records, a current medication list, and attendant contact details. Before flying home, request operative notes, imaging, and medication lists so follow-up with your doctor in Iraq is straightforward.

## Transplant and high-acuity pathways

For kidney or liver transplant evaluation, donor documentation and legal compatibility must be confirmed early.
