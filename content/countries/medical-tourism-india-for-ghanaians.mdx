## Medical treatment in India for Ghanaian patients

Patients from Accra, Kumasi, Takoradi, and across Ghana travel to India for complex specialty care that may involve long waits or higher private costs at home. TechdrHealth matches Ghanaian patients with accredited hospitals and clarifies package inclusions before you fly.

## Popular care pathways for Ghana

Oncology, cardiac surgery, joint replacement, and IVF are among the most requested pathways. Families often compare private Accra quotes with India packages that bundle surgeon, theatre, and stay — then decide based on specialist depth and total cost of travel plus treatment.

We compare doctors and hospitals against your reports so you can choose clinical fit, not a fixed referral list. Ask early about what investigations are included, how many hospital nights are covered, and what happens if ICU days extend.

## Travel and visa notes from Ghana

Flights from Accra typically connect through a regional or Gulf hub into Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, or Chennai. After clinical review, a hospital invitation letter supports your medical visa application; attendants can usually apply alongside the patient.

Budget for attendant lodging, local transport, and a buffer for extended stay if recovery is slower than expected. Packages are quoted in USD with an approximate GHS planning reference.
