## Medical treatment in India for British patients

British patients explore India when NHS waits are long, private UK quotes are high, or a specialist pathway is clearer at a high-volume Indian centre. TechdrHealth provides hospital comparisons, package clarity, and travel coordination without pressure — so you can weigh clinical fit, not marketing lists.

Typical journeys start with an email enquiry or form, remote review of reports, a shortlist of accredited hospitals, and an indicative USD package range. Only then do families commit to visa paperwork and flights.

## NHS waits, private UK costs, and when India becomes practical

Elective orthopedics, dental implants, IVF, spine, cancer, and some cardiac pathways are where British patients most often compare India. An all-in India package can include surgeon fees, theatre, implants or devices (as quoted), hospital stay, and basic attendant logistics.

India is not a substitute for emergency NHS care. It is a planned option for elective or semi-elective specialty treatment when you want shorter waits and transparent package economics at JCI or NABH hospitals.

## Practical planning from the UK

Direct and one-stop flights into Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, and Hyderabad make short treatment trips feasible. We align hospital dates with visa validity, flight constraints, and attendant hotel stay near campus.

Pack digital and printed records, a current medication list, and contact details for your UK clinician. After discharge, request operative notes and imaging before you fly home so continuity of care is straightforward.

## Continuity with your GP or specialist

British patients often ask whether Indian hospitals will communicate clearly with a UK GP after discharge. We treat that as part of the package conversation — request notes, implant cards, and imaging in formats your home clinician can use.
