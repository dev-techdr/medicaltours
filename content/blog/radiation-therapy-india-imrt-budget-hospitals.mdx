## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is IMRT in India the same standard as the US?**
Accredited centres use calibrated linear accelerators and follow international fractionation guidelines. Confirm technique (IMRT vs 3D-CRT) in your written quote.

**How long must I stay in India for a full radiation course?**
Conventional courses: 5–7 weeks of daily treatment. SBRT: fewer days. Your quote should state total calendar time including simulation.

**Can I compare radiation-only at one hospital and surgery at another?**
Possible but logistically harder. Coordinated care at one centre is usually smoother — ask coordinators after review.

**Does the package include planning scans and masks?**
Reputable packages include simulation, planning, and standard immobilisation. Confirm inclusions line by line.
