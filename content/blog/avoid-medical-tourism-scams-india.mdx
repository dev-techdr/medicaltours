Avoid facilitators who refuse written quotes, pressure immediate payment to personal accounts, or cannot name specific accredited hospitals and doctors. Always verify hospital accreditation, request itemized packages, and keep communication on official channels. A reliable medical tourism company will have clear hospital MOUs, transparent pricing, and reachable local support — like TechdrHealth in Hyderabad.
