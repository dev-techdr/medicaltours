Immunotherapy has changed outcomes for many advanced cancers — but Western pricing puts checkpoint inhibitors out of reach for self-pay families. India offers **the same class of drugs** (originator or approved biosimilars) at **accredited oncology centres**, with **budget comparisons across hospitals** and **video consultation** to confirm eligibility before travel.

## Video consultation: confirm IO eligibility remotely

Immunotherapy is not appropriate for all patients. A **video consultation** with the medical oncologist covers:

- Biomarker requirements (PD-L1, EGFR status for lung, etc.)
- Expected response and monitoring schedule
- Side-effect management and when to seek urgent care
- Whether IO alone or combined with chemo is planned

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is immunotherapy in India the same drug as in the US?**
Major centres use originator drugs or regulator-approved alternatives. Confirm exact drug name and manufacturer in your written quote.

**Can I continue immunotherapy started abroad in India?**
Often yes if records and drug availability align — share full infusion history for review.

**How many hospital quotes will I receive?**
Typically two to four when multiple centres can deliver your protocol.

**Does insurance cover immunotherapy done in India?**
Depends on your home policy. Hospital provides documentation; you submit to insurer.
