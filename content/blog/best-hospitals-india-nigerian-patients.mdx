India has become the leading medical tourism destination for Nigerian patients, ahead of the US and UK, largely due to significantly lower costs paired with accredited hospital standards. Nigerian patients most commonly travel to India for cardiac surgery, cancer treatment, orthopedic procedures, and fertility care.

## Why Nigerian Patients Choose India

- **Cost savings of 50–80%** compared to equivalent private treatment in the US or UK
- **JCI and NABH-accredited hospitals** with internationally trained surgeons
- **High surgical volumes** across cardiac, oncology, and orthopedic specialties
- **Faster access to treatment** — no long waiting lists for scheduling
- **Established patient pathways** — direct flight connections and existing Nigerian patient communities at major hospital hubs

## Common Treatments Nigerian Patients Travel For

**Cardiac care:** Heart bypass surgery (CABG), valve replacement, and angioplasty are among the most requested procedures, typically arranged in Hyderabad, Chennai, or Delhi cardiac centers.

**Cancer treatment:** Chemotherapy, radiation, and surgical oncology — with access to both branded and biosimilar drug protocols at a fraction of Western pricing.

**Orthopedics:** Knee and hip replacement using internationally recognized implant brands.

**Fertility treatment:** IVF cycles at accredited fertility centers, often more affordable than options within Nigeria or the Gulf.

## What to Look for in a Hospital

When evaluating hospitals for treatment in India, Nigerian patients should confirm:
- **Accreditation status** (JCI and/or NABH) — ask for the certificate directly
- **Surgeon experience** with your specific procedure and case complexity
- **Dedicated international patient department** with interpreter and coordination support
- **Transparent, itemized cost estimates** before committing to travel

## Visa and Travel Process for Nigerian Patients

Nigerian patients require a **Medical Visa (M-Visa)**, which typically needs a hospital invitation letter, medical reports, and proof of financial means. Processing generally takes a few business days once documents are submitted. A patient care coordinator can help prepare and submit the required documentation.

## What to Expect During Your Trip

1. **Report review** — medical records reviewed remotely to shortlist suitable hospitals and get a cost estimate
2. **Travel and visa coordination** — invitation letter, flight guidance, and airport pickup arranged
3. **Treatment and hospital stay** — admission, procedure, and recovery with interpreter support as needed
4. **Follow-up care** — discharge instructions and remote follow-up after returning to Nigeria
