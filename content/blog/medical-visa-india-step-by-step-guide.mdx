If you're traveling to India for treatment, you'll need a **Medical Visa (M-Visa)** — not a standard tourist visa. Here's exactly what's required and how the process works.

## What Is an India Medical Visa (M-Visa)?

The M-Visa is a special visa category issued specifically for foreign nationals traveling to India for medical treatment. It allows longer stays than a tourist visa and can be extended locally if treatment requires it, and permits up to two accompanying attendants under a linked **Medical Attendant Visa (MX-Visa)**.

## Documents You'll Need

- **Valid passport** with at least 6 months validity remaining
- **Hospital invitation/recommendation letter** from the treating hospital in India, stating the diagnosis and proposed treatment
- **Medical reports** supporting the need for treatment
- **Proof of financial means** to cover treatment and stay
- **Passport-size photographs** (per Indian visa photo specifications)
- **Completed online visa application form**

For attendants: a copy of the patient's visa approval and proof of relationship to the patient.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Get a hospital confirmation.** Before applying, you need a firm treatment plan and invitation letter from an Indian hospital — this is often coordinated through a medical tourism facilitator alongside your treatment estimate.
2. **Complete the online application** via the Indian visa portal for your country of residence.
3. **Submit documents** including medical reports and the hospital invitation letter.
4. **Attend biometrics/appointment** if required by your local Indian visa application center.
5. **Receive visa approval** — processing typically takes a few business days, though this varies by country and case urgency.
6. **Book travel** once the visa is confirmed.

## How Long Does It Take?

Standard processing is typically **3–5 business days** after document submission, though this varies by country of application and case urgency. Urgent medical cases can sometimes be expedited — flag this clearly when applying.

## Attendant Visas

Patients are generally permitted **up to two attendants** on a linked MX-Visa, useful for a family member or caregiver accompanying the patient throughout treatment and recovery.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Applying with a tourist visa** — treatment cannot legally proceed under a tourist visa; hospitals may refuse non-emergency procedures without a valid M-Visa.
- **Incomplete medical documentation** — missing or vague medical reports are a common cause of delays.
- **Not confirming attendant visa needs early** — apply for both simultaneously if a caregiver is traveling with you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I convert a tourist visa to a medical visa once in India?**
This is not a reliable option — apply for the correct M-Visa before traveling.

**How long can I stay on a medical visa?**
Duration depends on the treatment plan; M-Visas can be extended locally through the Foreigners Regional Registration Office (FRRO) if treatment requires a longer stay than initially granted.

**Do children need a separate visa?**
Yes, each traveler — including children — requires their own visa application, generally under the attendant (MX) category if accompanying a patient.
