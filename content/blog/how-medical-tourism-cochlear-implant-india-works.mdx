## Phase 1: Initial Inquiry and Assessment — Week 1

### Step 1: Share Your Reports

Send documents by [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/916303225006?text=Hi%2C%20I%20want%20to%20understand%20how%20cochlear%20implant%20medical%20tourism%20to%20India%20works) or the [consultation form](/contact-us#enquiry-form):

- Audiological reports: **ABR**, ASSR, and a pure tone audiogram if available
- OAE (otoacoustic emissions) result
- Previous ENT notes or hearing aid trial history
- Age and a short medical history

> **Don't have all of these?** The clinical team will say which tests are still needed. Those can be arranged on arrival in India.

### Step 2: Free Medical Review (Within 24 Hours)

A partner ENT surgeon reviews the reports within **24–48 hours** and sends:

- A preliminary opinion on candidacy
- A recommended hospital and surgeon for the case
- An itemised **cost estimate** with no hidden charges

| Item | What the estimate shows |
|---|---|
| Surgeon, hospital, and anaesthesia | Listed separately |
| Device cost by brand | Cochlear, Advanced Bionics, and MED-EL options |
| Pre-operative investigations | CT, MRI, audiology |
| Switch-on and mapping sessions | Included in the estimate |
| Medical Tours India coordination fee | Fixed and stated upfront |

## Phase 2: Planning and Logistics — Week 2–3

### Step 3: Medical Visa

India issues a **medical visa** for treatment at recognised hospitals. Medical Tours India provides:

- A formal **hospital invitation letter**
- A **facilitator cover letter**, which most embassies ask for
- Guidance for your country's Indian Embassy or High Commission

Processing is typically **3–7 business days** from the application date. We start as soon as you confirm the booking. The full sequence is on the [medical visa assistance](/medical-visa-assistance) page.

## Phase 3: Treatment in India — Days 1–14

### Day 1: Airport Arrival

Your **Medical Tours India coordinator** meets you at arrivals, goes with you to the accommodation, and walks through the schedule. You also have an orientation call with the hospital's international patient team the same day.

### Days 1–2: Pre-Operative Workup

| Investigation | Purpose |
|---|---|
| High-resolution CT of the temporal bone | Cochlear anatomy and ossification |
| MRI of the internal acoustic meatus | Cochlear nerve integrity |
| Comprehensive audiology | Candidacy and baseline hearing |
| Paediatric medical clearance | Anaesthesia risk |
| Surgeon consultation | Imaging review and surgical plan |

### Day 3–4: Surgery Day

Cochlear implant surgery takes **2–3 hours under general anaesthesia**. Family wait in the hospital lounge and get updates from the nursing team. Most children are awake and eating within a few hours. The post-operative stay is **4–5 nights**.

### Day 8–10: Device Switch-On (Activation)

The audiologist activates the external speech processor and programmes the **first MAP**. Follow-up mapping around days 12 and 14 refines the settings before you leave.

### Days 10–14: Follow-Up Mapping and Discharge

Before you leave India, you receive:

- The **discharge summary** and operative report
- The device **manual, warranty cards**, and a spare-parts kit
- A first-month **home auditory verbal therapy guide**
- Scheduled **teleconsultation dates** with your Indian audiologist
- The local service-centre contact for your implant brand

## Phase 4: Return Home and Long-Term Follow-Up

### Remote Mapping

Most cochlear implant manufacturers support **remote mapping**. Your Indian audiologist can adjust programming in a secure online session, so a return trip is not required for routine changes. We set this up before you leave.

### Medical Tours India Stays Your Case Manager

Medical Tours India remains the case manager after discharge. Families still write with follow-up questions years later, and those messages are answered.
