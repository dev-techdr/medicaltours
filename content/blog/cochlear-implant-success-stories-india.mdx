## Story 1: Ibrahim — 16 Months | Congenital Deafness | Nigeria to Hyderabad

Ibrahim's parents learned he had **profound bilateral hearing loss** at a 6-month check in Lagos. After trying to access implantation in Nigeria, where waiting lists and cost blocked treatment, they found Medical Tours India through a Facebook group for families of children with hearing loss in West Africa.

> "We sent our audiologist's reports on a Wednesday. By Friday, Medical Tours India had arranged a free consultation with the ENT surgeon. Within three weeks, we were in Hyderabad."

Ibrahim had a **unilateral cochlear implant at 16 months**. The family says the switch-on, when he heard his mother's voice, was shared 14,000 times in hearing-loss groups online. He is now three, attends a mainstream nursery, and his **speech intelligibility scores are in the normal range for his age**.

## Story 2: Amira — 34 Years | Post-Meningitis Deafness | UAE to Chennai

Amira was a secondary school teacher in Dubai when **bacterial meningitis left her profoundly deaf in both ears** at 32. Hearing aids for 18 months brought little benefit. Her ENT in Dubai recommended implantation and quoted about AED 280,000 (about USD 76,000) for bilateral surgery.

A colleague connected her with Medical Tours India. Her imaging showed **early ossification in the left cochlea**, so the case was fast-tracked to a centre in Chennai experienced with post-meningitis implants.

> "The surgeon explained that if I waited another 3–4 months, bilateral implantation might not be possible because of the ossification. Within 10 days, I was in surgery."

Amira received **bilateral cochlear implants in one admission**. She has returned to full-time teaching.

## Story 3: Emmanuel — 4 Years | Auditory Neuropathy | Kenya to Bangalore

Emmanuel had **auditory neuropathy spectrum disorder (ANSD)**: the cochlea is intact, but the signal to the brain is disrupted. Hearing aids are often a poor fit.

Two specialists in Nairobi had said he was not a candidate. A video opinion from a partner surgeon in Bangalore disagreed. A dedicated ANSD workup, including **cortical auditory evoked potentials (CAEP)**, supported implantation.

> "We were told no twice. Medical Tours India gave us hope and then proof. Emmanuel's surgery was done by a surgeon who had performed over 400 cochlear implants."

Emmanuel is now in Grade 1 in a **mainstream school**, with a classroom FM system. His teacher reports that he joins classroom activities.

## Story 4: Rahma — 8 Months | Genetic Deafness | Tanzania to Hyderabad

Rahma's hearing loss was found at **birth on newborn screening** at a mission hospital in Dar es Salaam. Connexin 26 testing confirmed a hereditary cause. Her parents started looking at implants at 4 months, because earlier surgery supports language development. The children's pathway is covered in the [guide for international parents](/blog/cochlear-implant-children-india-guide).

Medical Tours India arranged the pre-operative review by teleconsultation. She was implanted at **8 months and 10 days** at a partner hospital experienced with very young children.

> "We trusted Medical Tours India because they knew which hospital had experience with such young children. They did not send us to the biggest or most expensive hospital — they sent us to the right one."

At age 2, her **speech development was tracking normal milestones** on the assessments the family shared.

## Story 5: Abdul — 62 Years | Age-Related Deafness and Tinnitus | Bangladesh to Hyderabad

Abdul is a retired civil servant from Dhaka. He used increasingly powerful hearing aids for a decade before his audiologist concluded he was a cochlear implant candidate.

His main complaint, alongside hearing loss, was **severe tinnitus** that had disrupted sleep and concentration for five years. Many adults report less tinnitus after implantation. Abdul's trip from Dhaka to Hyderabad was short, and he was home within 12 days.

> "Before surgery I could not sleep for more than 3 hours. The noise in my head was constant. Three months after activation, the tinnitus is almost completely gone. My wife says I am a different man."
