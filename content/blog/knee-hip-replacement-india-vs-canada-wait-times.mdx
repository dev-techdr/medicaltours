Provincial wait-lists for elective joint replacement are one of the most common reasons Canadian patients explore India. This guide compares how families weigh wait time at home against accredited Indian packages — and when travel is worth considering.

## Why Canadians look abroad for joints

Many Canadians are quoted months (sometimes longer) for elective hip or knee replacement. Private or out-of-province options can be expensive. India offers high-volume orthopedic centres with JCI or NABH accreditation and bundled packages that include surgeon fees, theatre, implants as quoted, and hospital stay.

India does not replace emergency Canadian care. It is a planned elective pathway when wait-lists or private economics favour travelling.

## Typical cost ranges (USD)

| Pathway | India (typical package) | Canada context |
|---|---|---|
| Knee replacement | Often a fraction of private North American cash-pay | Public wait + possible private/self-pay alternatives |
| Hip replacement | Similar package structure to knee | Same wait-list dynamic for many elective cases |

Exact numbers depend on implant choice, hospital, and clinical complexity. Packages are quoted in USD with a CAD planning reference. Use our [cost calculator](/cost-calculator) and [knee](/cost-comparison/total-knee-replacement-surgery-india) or [hip](/treatments) cost pages for current ranges.

## What to compare before you book

- Accreditation (JCI / NABH) and surgeon volume for your implant type
- What the package includes and excludes (ICU days, physiotherapy, attendant lodging)
- Expected stay length and when it is safe to fly home
- Discharge notes and imaging for your Canadian clinician
