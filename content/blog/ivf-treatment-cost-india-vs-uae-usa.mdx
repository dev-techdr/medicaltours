In vitro fertilization (IVF) helps couples facing infertility through controlled ovarian stimulation, egg retrieval, lab fertilization, and embryo transfer. Cost per cycle is one of the biggest factors driving international couples toward India.

## Quick Cost Comparison (Per IVF Cycle)

| Country | Typical Cost (USD) |
|---|---|
| India | $2,500 – $5,500 |
| UAE | $8,000 – $12,000 |
| USA | $15,000 – $25,000 |

*Costs rise with add-ons like ICSI, PGT genetic testing, donor eggs/sperm, or frozen embryo transfer cycles.*

## Why Does IVF Cost Less in India?

- **Lower lab and clinic operating costs**, without a drop in embryology standards at accredited fertility centers.
- **Experienced fertility specialists** — India's leading IVF centers perform high cycle volumes annually, giving embryology teams substantial hands-on experience.
- **Transparent per-cycle packages** rather than the itemized, insurance-driven billing common in the US.
- **Shorter wait times** for cycle start compared to some Western clinics with longer scheduling queues.

## What's Typically Included in an IVF Package

- Initial consultation and fertility assessment
- Ovarian stimulation medication protocol (monitored)
- Egg retrieval procedure
- Lab fertilization (standard IVF or ICSI, depending on package)
- Embryo transfer
- Basic hormonal support post-transfer

**Often separate:** genetic testing (PGT-A/PGT-M), donor egg/sperm fees, embryo freezing/storage, and multiple cycle discounts — ask for these as line items.

## How Long Should You Plan to Stay?

A single fresh IVF cycle typically requires **2–3 weeks in India**, covering stimulation monitoring, retrieval, and transfer. Some couples opt to freeze embryos and return later for transfer, which shortens the initial trip.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are IVF success rates in India comparable to the US or UAE?**
At accredited centers with experienced embryology teams, success rates are generally comparable for equivalent age groups and diagnoses — ask any clinic for their published clinic-specific success rates, not just national averages.

**Is donor egg/sperm IVF legal for foreign patients in India?**
Regulations exist under Indian law and vary by patient circumstances — this needs to be confirmed directly with the clinic based on your specific case before travel.

**Can we do multiple cycles in one trip?**
Typically no — one fresh cycle per trip is standard, though frozen embryo transfer in a follow-up visit is common.
