## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long stay after robotic prostatectomy in India?**
Often 7–14 days in India; catheter duration and flight clearance depend on surgeon advice — typically 10–14 days minimum.

**Can I get IMRT and hormone therapy at a budget hospital?**
Yes at centres like Medicover and AOI — confirm full-course inclusions in writing.

**Do I need a multiparametric MRI before quote?**
MRI and biopsy Gleason grading strongly improve plan accuracy. Share all available staging.

**Can I compare surgery at one hospital and radiation at another?**
Possible but coordinated single-centre care is often simpler. Coordinators advise after review.
