Joint replacement and spine surgery are among the most common reasons international patients choose Hyderabad, and for good reason — the city has developed deep orthopaedic expertise at a fraction of Western cost.

- [Total and partial knee replacement](/treatments/orthopaedic-treatments-india/total-knee-replacement-surgery-india)

These pathways are available at NABH-accredited Hyderabad partners including [Olive Hospitals](/hospital-network/olive-hospitals), [TX Hospitals](/hospital-network/tx-hospitals), and [Medicover Hospitals](/hospital-network/medicover-hospitals).

## Why Implant Quality Matters

Ask specifically which implant brands a hospital uses — reputable Hyderabad hospitals use the same internationally certified implant manufacturers (Zimmer Biomet, Stryker, DePuy, and similar) used in US and UK hospitals, not lower-cost unbranded alternatives. This is a fair and important question to ask directly.

## Recovery Timeline for International Patients

Most joint replacement patients need 10–14 days in-country before flying home, including initial physiotherapy. Hospitals coordinate discharge planning and often connect patients with physiotherapy referrals in their home country for continued recovery. See [travel and stay support](/travel-accommodation-assistance) for recovery housing near hospitals.

## Who's a Good Candidate for Traveling for Orthopaedic Surgery

Patients facing long public healthcare wait times (common in the [UK](/countries/medical-tourism-india-for-uk-patients), [Canada](/countries/medical-tourism-india-for-canadian-patients), and parts of the Gulf) or high out-of-pocket private costs (common in the [US](/countries/medical-tourism-india-for-usa-patients)) are increasingly choosing Hyderabad as a faster, lower-cost path to the same procedure. Wait-time context: [knee and hip replacement India vs Canada](/blog/knee-hip-replacement-india-vs-canada-wait-times).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which Hyderabad hospitals do knee and hip replacements for foreigners?**
NABH-accredited partners such as Olive, TX, and Medicover Hospitals routinely treat international orthopaedic patients.

**Do Hyderabad hospitals use branded implants?**
Reputable centres use internationally certified brands such as Zimmer Biomet, Stryker, and DePuy. Always confirm the implant in your written quote.

**How long do I need to stay after knee replacement?**
Typically 10–14 days in Hyderabad, including early physiotherapy, before flying home.

**Can I get both knees done in one trip?**
Bilateral replacement is possible for eligible patients. A specialist review of your reports determines whether a single trip is appropriate.
