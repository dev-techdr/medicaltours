Dental full-arch rehab and elective cosmetic surgery are among the most common elective reasons Australian and British patients travel to India. Transparent stage pricing and short coordinated stays matter as much as the headline savings.

## What usually drives the decision

- Private implant quotes at home that feel unreachable
- Desire to complete staged dental work in one planned trip
- Cosmetic packages with clear surgeon fees and hospital nights as quoted

Savings are often substantial versus private Australia/UK pricing — but only after you confirm accreditation, surgeon case volume, implant brand, and what follow-up visits are included.

## Planning tips

- Ask for staged timelines (extraction, grafting, implant placement, final prosthesis)
- Confirm hotel nights near campus and when flying home is advised
- Request written discharge notes for your home dentist or GP
