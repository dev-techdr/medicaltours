Uncertainty about "how this actually works" is one of the biggest barriers for patients considering treatment abroad. Here's the process, made explicit. The same journey is outlined on [How It Works](/how-it-works). For a city-level walkthrough, see [what to expect during treatment in Hyderabad](/blog/patient-journey-medical-treatment-hyderabad).

## Step 1 — Free Inquiry and Report Review

Patients submit medical reports through the [website](/contact-us#enquiry-form) or [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/916303225006); a specialist reviews the case, typically within 24–48 hours.

## Step 2 — Written Treatment Plan and Cost Estimate

Patients receive a specific, itemized plan — procedure, hospital, estimated cost, and timeline — before any commitment. [Get a free quote](/get-free-quote) is the dedicated entry point for this step.

## Step 3 — Optional Free Second Opinion

Patients uncertain about the recommended treatment can request an independent specialist review at no cost. [How and why that works](/blog/free-second-opinion-before-traveling-india).

## Step 4 — Visa and Travel Coordination

Once a patient confirms they want to proceed, Medical Tours India coordinates the hospital invitation letter, visa documentation guidance, and travel/accommodation arrangements via [medical visa assistance](/medical-visa-assistance) and [travel support](/travel-accommodation-assistance).

## Step 5 — Arrival and Treatment

Patients are met, supported through pre-procedure evaluation, and accompanied through treatment with case management and [interpreter support](/interpreter-language-support) as needed.

## Step 6 — Recovery and Discharge Planning

A written recovery plan is provided before discharge, designed for continuity with the patient's home-country doctor.

## Step 7 — Post-Return Follow-Up

Continued contact after the patient returns home, distinguishing a genuine long-term care relationship from a one-time transaction.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long from first inquiry to a written plan?**
Typically 24–48 hours after reports are complete enough for review.

**Do I have to commit before I see costs?**
No. The written treatment plan and estimate come before any commitment to travel or payment.

**When does visa work start?**
After you confirm you want to proceed. The hospital invitation letter and document checklist are coordinated at that point — not before you have a plan.

**Is follow-up included after I go home?**
Yes. Remote contact after return is part of the process, including coordination with your local doctor when needed.
