## What Makes a Great Cochlear Implant Centre?

This is what we evaluate before suggesting a centre:

- **Annual surgical volume** — ideally 100+ cochlear implants per year
- **Dedicated paediatric ENT and audiology team**
- **On-site auditory verbal therapy (AVT) or speech therapy**
- **Intraoperative NRT** (Neural Response Telemetry)
- **International patient services** with language support
- **NABH and/or JCI accreditation**
- **All three implant brands** available (Cochlear, Advanced Bionics, MED-EL)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are Indian hospitals as good as hospitals in the UK or USA for cochlear implants?**
For cochlear implant surgery, India's top ENT centres have equal or higher surgical volumes than most UK or US centres. The technology is the same implant brands and the same intraoperative monitoring. The main differences are cost and waiting time.

**Is there an international patient desk at these hospitals?**
Yes. The centres we use have international patient departments experienced with patients from Africa, the Middle East, and South Asia. Medical visa invitation letters, foreign-currency billing, and interpreter services are standard.

**What happens if there's a complication after I return home?**
Serious complications from cochlear implant surgery are rare. Device failures are covered by manufacturer warranty globally. Your Indian surgeon remains available by teleconsultation, and Medical Tours India stays your case manager after you return home.
