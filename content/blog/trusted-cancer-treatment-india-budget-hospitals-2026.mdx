## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I know a cancer hospital in India is trustworthy?**
Look for JCI or NABH accreditation, named oncology programmes, tumour-board review for complex cases, and written packages — not verbal quotes. Medical Tours India only shortlists verified partner hospitals.

**Can I compare budget options from several hospitals?**
Yes. After report review, you typically receive two to four hospital options with indicative package ranges so you can compare cost, city, and specialist fit before choosing.

**Is video consultation with an Indian oncologist free?**
Initial report review and second opinion are free. Video consultation with a hospital specialist may be arranged when clinically appropriate — coordinators confirm any hospital-specific fees before scheduling.

**How much cheaper is cancer treatment in India than the US or UK?**
Many protocols cost 60–85% less than US self-pay and 40–65% less than UK private care, depending on cancer type and drug regimen. Personalised ranges come after report review.

**Do I have to travel immediately after the video consult?**
No. Video consult helps you decide timing. Many families review the plan with a home oncologist before booking flights.
