Cancer care decisions should start with clinical clarity, not flights. For many US and UK families, India becomes relevant after a remote second opinion clarifies whether an accredited Indian pathway fits the diagnosis, staging, and goals of care.

## Start with reports, not tickets

Share pathology, staging scans, prior treatment summaries, and current medications. Medical Tours India helps shortlist hospitals and specialists and outlines indicative package ranges — usually within 24–48 hours when reports are complete. Complex cases may need an extra specialist cycle.

Request a [free second opinion](/free-second-opinion).

## Quality bar for Western oncology travellers

- JCI / NABH partner hospitals with documented oncology programmes
- Clear inclusions (investigations, chemo cycles as quoted, ICU if relevant)
- Discharge documentation for your home oncologist or NHS/GP team
- Realistic stay length and attendant support
