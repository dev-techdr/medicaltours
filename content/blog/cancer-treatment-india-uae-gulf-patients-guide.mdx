## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is cancer treatment in India halal-certified or culturally sensitive for Gulf patients?**
Hospitals accommodate dietary and privacy preferences. Request specific needs during enquiry — coordinators note them for the international desk.

**Can I pay in USD and get a formal invoice for insurance?**
Most international packages are quoted in USD with itemised hospital invoices suitable for reimbursement review (subject to your policy).

**How fast can I get quotes from multiple hospitals?**
Typically 24–48 hours after complete pathology and imaging are shared.

**Do I need to visit India before starting chemo?**
Report review and video consult happen remotely. First chemo cycle requires in-person admission unless your case allows oral therapy start — oncologist confirms.
