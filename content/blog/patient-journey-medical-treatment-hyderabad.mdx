Traveling abroad for surgery is a significant decision, and much of the anxiety around it comes from not knowing what the process actually looks like. Here's a realistic walkthrough. You can also follow the operational steps on [How It Works](/how-it-works).

## Step 1: Initial Consultation

Patients share medical reports and history, typically reviewed by a specialist within 24–48 hours, resulting in a written treatment plan and cost estimate — before any commitment or travel. Start with a [free second opinion](/free-second-opinion) or [enquiry form](/contact-us#enquiry-form).

## Step 2: Visa and Travel Arrangements

Once a hospital and procedure are confirmed, the hospital's international desk (or a facilitator) issues the documentation needed for the medical visa, and travel/accommodation is arranged, often including airport pickup. See the [medical visa for Hyderabad guide](/blog/medical-visa-hyderabad-india-guide-2026) and [travel & accommodation assistance](/travel-accommodation-assistance).

## Step 3: Arrival and Pre-Procedure Evaluation

Most hospitals conduct a same- or next-day in-person evaluation confirming the treatment plan before proceeding, allowing patients to ask questions directly with their surgical team.

## Step 4: Procedure and Hospital Stay

Hospital stay length varies significantly by procedure — from same-day for minor procedures to 1–2 weeks for complex surgery — with case managers coordinating communication for patients and accompanying family. [Interpreter support](/interpreter-language-support) is available when needed.

## Step 5: Recovery and Discharge Planning

Before discharge, hospitals provide a written recovery plan, medication guidance, and follow-up instructions designed to be usable once the patient returns home, often coordinating with a local doctor for continued care.

## Step 6: Post-Return Follow-Up

Reputable facilitators and hospitals maintain contact after the patient returns home — a detail worth specifically asking about before choosing where to be treated.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How quickly can I get a treatment plan for Hyderabad?**
Specialists typically review reports within 24–48 hours and return a written plan and estimate before you book travel.

**Is airport pickup included?**
Often yes when coordinated through a facilitator or the hospital international desk. Confirm this in your travel plan.

**Do I decide on surgery before I arrive?**
The remote plan is confirmed with an in-person evaluation after arrival. Surgery proceeds only once you and the surgical team agree.

**What happens after I fly home?**
You should leave with a written recovery plan and a follow-up channel with the hospital or facilitator. Ask how post-return contact works before you book.
