Securing a medical visa is often the first real hurdle for international patients planning treatment in Hyderabad. Here's the process, simplified. For the country-wide version, see our [India medical visa step-by-step guide](/blog/medical-visa-india-step-by-step-guide).

## Who Needs a Medical Visa

Any foreign national traveling to India specifically for medical treatment should apply for an M-Visa (Medical Visa), not a standard tourist visa. India also issues an MX-Visa for attendants accompanying the patient.

## Required Documents

- A letter from the treating Hyderabad hospital confirming the procedure and estimated duration of stay
- Passport with at least six months' validity
- Recent medical reports
- Proof of sufficient funds

Most Hyderabad hospital international-patient desks will prepare the hospital invitation letter directly. Our team can also [coordinate visa documentation](/medical-visa-assistance).

## Processing Timeline

M-Visas are typically processed within 3–5 business days once the hospital documentation is submitted, though this varies by embassy/consulate location and nationality. Applying at least 2–3 weeks before planned travel is recommended to account for scheduling.

## Extensions for Extended Treatment

Medical visas can typically be extended locally through the Foreigners Regional Registration Office (FRRO) if treatment or recovery requires a longer stay than initially planned — common for transplant, cancer, or multi-stage orthopaedic cases.

## Where Facilitators Help

A good medical tourism facilitator in Hyderabad will handle the hospital invitation letter, coordinate with the hospital's international desk, and guide documentation — reducing what can otherwise be a confusing bureaucratic process to a few clear steps. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I enter India on a tourist visa for planned surgery in Hyderabad?**
No. Planned treatment should be on an M-Visa. Hospitals may refuse non-emergency procedures without a valid medical visa.

**How long does a medical visa for Hyderabad take in 2026?**
Typically 3–5 business days after hospital documents are submitted, though embassy location and nationality can extend this. Apply 2–3 weeks before travel.

**Can a family member travel with me?**
Yes. Accompanying attendants apply for an MX-Visa linked to the patient's M-Visa.

**Who issues the hospital invitation letter?**
The treating Hyderabad hospital's international desk, usually after a treatment plan is confirmed. A facilitator can coordinate this for you.
