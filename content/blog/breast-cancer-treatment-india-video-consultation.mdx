Breast cancer pathways vary widely — lumpectomy vs mastectomy, sentinel node biopsy, reconstruction timing, and adjuvant chemo or hormone therapy. India offers **accredited surgical and medical oncology programmes at a fraction of Western pricing**, with the ability to **compare multiple hospitals** and speak to a specialist via **video consultation** before booking flights.

## Typical breast cancer cost ranges (USD, indicative)

| Component | India | USA (self-pay) | UK (private) |

Chemo and radiation are often quoted **per session / full course** the same way as other solid tumours — see the AOI line-item format in [trusted cancer treatment](/blog/trusted-cancer-treatment-india-budget-hospitals-2026) (investigations ~$500–$600; RT full course commonly ~$4,000–$7,000).

## Why video consultation matters for breast cancer

Breast cancer decisions are deeply personal — surgery type, reconstruction timing, and fertility preservation if relevant. A **video consultation** with the surgical or medical oncologist lets you:

- Discuss lumpectomy vs mastectomy rationale for *your* tumour profile
- Ask about oncoplastic techniques and reconstruction options
- Confirm adjuvant therapy sequence before committing to travel
- Involve a family member or home doctor on the call

Available when reports are complete. Start with [free second opinion](/free-second-opinion).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I get breast reconstruction in India during the same trip?**
Immediate or delayed reconstruction depends on cancer stage and hospital capability. Max Healthcare Delhi and select Hyderabad centres coordinate reconstruction — confirm in your quote.

**How long do I need to stay in India for breast cancer surgery?**
Surgery alone: often 7–14 days. With adjuvant chemo or radiation, stay may extend to several weeks — your written plan will state realistic timelines.

**Is video consultation available before choosing a hospital?**
Yes, when clinically appropriate after report review. You can also compare written plans only if you prefer.

**Are Indian breast cancer outcomes comparable to Western centres?**
Accredited centres with high surgical volumes and tumour-board review follow international guidelines. Ask for centre experience with your specific subtype in the video consult.
