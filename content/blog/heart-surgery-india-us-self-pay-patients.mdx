Uninsured and high-deductible Americans often discover that cash-pay cardiac quotes at home dwarf a bundled India package at an accredited centre. This guide covers how US patients evaluate heart surgery in India without leading on “cheap” alone.

## Who this pathway fits

- Self-pay or underinsured patients with elective or semi-elective cardiac pathways
- Families comparing multiple US cash-pay quotes who want a written India alternative
- Patients willing to plan travel after a remote report review

Emergency cardiac care should be treated locally. India is for planned specialty pathways after imaging and reports are reviewed.

## Cost and quality — in that order of decision-making

Savings of 60–80% versus many US list prices are common for CABG and related pathways at high-volume centres — but only after you verify JCI/NABH accreditation, surgeon volume, and package inclusions (ICU, ward, investigations, device costs).

Before you leave India, collect operative notes, imaging, and device or implant documentation. TechdrHealth treats this handoff as part of the plan so your US cardiologist or primary doctor can continue care.
