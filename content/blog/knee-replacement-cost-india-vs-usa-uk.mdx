Total knee arthroplasty replaces damaged joint surfaces with an implant to relieve pain and restore mobility. It's one of the most requested procedures among international patients traveling to India — largely due to the cost gap and shorter waiting times.

## Quick Cost Comparison

| Country | Typical Cost (per knee, USD) | Hospital Stay |
|---|---|---|
| India | $4,000 – $7,500 | 4–6 days |
| USA | $30,000 – $50,000 | 2–3 days |
| UK (private) | $15,000 – $22,000 | 3–4 days |

*Bilateral (both knees) procedures typically cost 60-80% of two separate single-knee prices when done in the same admission.*

## Why the Price Gap?

- **Implant sourcing:** India's high-volume orthopedic centers negotiate directly with global implant manufacturers (often the same US/EU brands used abroad), passing on lower procurement costs.
- **Lower facility overheads** compared to Western private hospitals.
- **Bundled packages** covering surgery, implant, hospital stay, and initial physiotherapy — versus itemized Western billing.
- **No long NHS-style waiting list** — private treatment in India is typically scheduled within days to a few weeks of diagnosis review.

## What's Included in a Package

- Pre-op assessment (X-rays, blood work, cardiac clearance if needed)
- Surgeon and OT fees
- Implant cost (brand varies by hospital — confirm which brand is quoted)
- Hospital stay (4–6 days typical)
- Initial physiotherapy sessions before discharge

**Not usually included:** flights, extended physiotherapy after discharge, mobility aids, or hotel stay for recovery period.

## Recovery Timeline

Most patients need **10–14 days in India** post-surgery before flying is advisable — allowing for wound healing and initial physiotherapy progress. Full recovery and return to normal activity typically takes 3–6 months, which continues after returning home.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What implant brands are used in India?**
Top hospitals commonly use internationally recognized brands (e.g., Zimmer Biomet, Stryker, DePuy Synthes) — always confirm the specific brand and warranty in your quote.

**Can I get both knees done at once?**
Bilateral replacement in a single admission is common in India and often more cost-efficient than two separate trips, though it depends on your fitness for surgery — your surgeon will assess this.

**How much can I save vs the US or UK?**
Typically 75–90% versus US pricing and 60–75% versus UK private pricing.
