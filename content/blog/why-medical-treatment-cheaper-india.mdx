Medical treatment in India costs less primarily because of lower labor and facility operating costs, high procedure volumes, competitive specialist markets, and favorable currency differentials — not because hospitals skip safety standards. Leading centers maintain JCI/NABH accreditation, modern equipment, and internationally trained doctors. Patients often save 50–80% versus the US or UK even after travel and accommodation.
