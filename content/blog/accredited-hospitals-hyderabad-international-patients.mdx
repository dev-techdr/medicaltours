Accreditation is the single most important safety signal for any patient considering [treatment abroad](/why-india-for-medical-treatment). Here's what it means, and which Hyderabad hospitals meet the standard.

## What JCI and NABH Actually Verify

Joint Commission International (JCI) accreditation is a globally recognized US-based standard evaluating patient safety protocols, infection control, and clinical outcomes. NABH is India's equivalent national standard. Hospitals holding either — ideally both — have been independently audited, not self-certified. See our [accreditations overview](/accreditations-certifications).

## What to Verify Before Booking

Ask any facilitator or hospital directly for:

- Current accreditation certificate
- The specific surgeon's credentials and experience with your procedure
- A written, itemized cost estimate — not just a verbal quote

Legitimate Hyderabad hospitals will provide all three without hesitation. For a practical checklist, see [how to choose a hospital in India for surgery](/blog/how-to-choose-hospital-india-surgery).

## Why This Matters More for International Patients

Domestic patients often rely on local reputation and word of mouth. International patients don't have that context — accreditation is the objective substitute, and it's worth treating as non-negotiable when researching where to have surgery in Hyderabad.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are Olive, TX, and Medicover hospitals accredited?**
Yes — these Hyderabad partner hospitals hold NABH accreditation. Always request the current certificate before travel, as accreditation is renewed periodically.

**Is JCI better than NABH?**
JCI is the global (US-based) standard; NABH is India's national equivalent. Either indicates independent audit. Many international patients treat one of the two as the minimum bar.

**How do I confirm a hospital is accredited?**
Ask for the current certificate, check the accreditation body's public listing, and work with a coordinator who will not proceed without documented status.
