## Written Cost Transparency vs Case-by-Case Negotiation

Some facilitators quote prices verbally and adjust based on perceived ability to pay. A written, itemized estimate protects patients from this — a standard practice worth confirming with any facilitator you're considering. [Get a free written quote](/get-free-quote).

Dedicated [country pages](/countries) covering visa requirements, cost expectations, and hospital recommendations specific to a patient's home country reflect a facilitator that has actually built process depth — not just a generic contact form behind a marketing site.
