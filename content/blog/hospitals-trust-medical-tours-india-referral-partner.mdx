Trust in medical tourism runs both directions — hospitals need to trust that facilitators are sending well-matched, well-prepared patients, just as patients need to trust the facilitator guiding them. Hospitals that want to join the network can apply via [hospital empanelment](/hospital-empanelment).

## Why Hospitals Are Selective About Referral Partners

Hospitals accepting international patients take on real coordination responsibility — visa documentation, language support, extended case management. They partner selectively with facilitators who prepare patients properly and communicate clearly, rather than working with anyone who sends inquiries.

## What Proper Preparation Looks Like

Complete medical reports shared upfront, realistic expectations set with the patient about cost and recovery, and clear communication about the patient's specific medical history — all before the patient ever arrives — make treatment smoother for both hospital and patient. That preparation is the same process patients see in [the Medical Tours India journey](/blog/medical-tours-india-patient-process-explained).

## The Empanelment Process

Hospitals typically evaluate a facilitator's track record, communication standards, and patient preparation quality before agreeing to a formal referral partnership — a vetting process that itself signals which facilitators hospitals consider reliable.

For Medical Tours India, the published steps are:

1. Submit hospital profile (accreditation, specialties, international-patient capability)
2. Partnerships review (clinical fit, language support, package readiness)
3. MOU and onboarding into the referral workflow

Named partners already in that model include [Olive Hospitals](/hospital-network/olive-hospitals), [TX Hospitals](/hospital-network/tx-hospitals), [Medicover Hospitals](/hospital-network/medicover-hospitals), and [Max Healthcare Delhi](/hospital-network/max-healthcare-delhi). Browse the [hospital network](/hospital-network).

## Why This Matters to Patients, Not Just Hospitals

A facilitator with genuine, long-standing hospital partnerships — rather than transactional, one-off referral arrangements — typically has more leverage to advocate for patients on cost, scheduling, and care quality, because the relationship is built on mutual trust rather than a single transaction.

That is one reason the [comparison of facilitators](/blog/medical-tours-india-vs-other-facilitators) treats *named, verifiable partnerships* as a patient-facing test, not a marketing line.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is hospital empanelment with Medical Tours India?**
A formal MOU partnership so the hospital joins the verified network for international referrals coordinated by Medical Tours India (TechdrHealth).

**Why should patients care about hospital–facilitator MOUs?**
MOUs mean the hospital has already vetted communication standards and patient preparation. Transactional, unnamed "partners" have not passed that filter.

**Can a hospital apply to join?**
Yes. Accredited centres with an international desk and transparent packages can [apply for empanelment](/hospital-empanelment). This is not government scheme empanelment (Aarogyasri, Ayushman Bharat, CGHS).

**Where do patients see partner hospitals?**
Featured partners appear in the [hospital network directory](/hospital-network). The wider 200+ network is matched case-by-case after medical review.
