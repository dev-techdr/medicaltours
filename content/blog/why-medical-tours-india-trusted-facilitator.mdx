Choosing to travel abroad for treatment requires trusting a facilitator you may never meet in person before arrival. Here's what Medical Tours India does differently.

## A Hyderabad-Based Team with Direct Hospital Relationships

Rather than acting as a generic referral service, Medical Tours India works directly with named partner hospitals across [Hyderabad](/hospital-network/hyderabad) and Delhi, allowing for faster, more accurate treatment planning and pricing — not guesswork. Meet the company on [About Us](/about-us) and the [team](/our-team).

Featured partners include [Olive Hospitals](/hospital-network/olive-hospitals), [TX Hospitals](/hospital-network/tx-hospitals), [Medicover Hospitals](/hospital-network/medicover-hospitals), and [Max Healthcare Delhi](/hospital-network/max-healthcare-delhi).

## Transparent, Written Packages from Day One

Every patient inquiry results in a written treatment plan and cost estimate, not a vague verbal quote designed to get someone to commit before they've seen real numbers. Start with a [free quote](/get-free-quote).

## Support That Doesn't End at the Hospital Door

[Medical visa assistance](/medical-visa-assistance), [travel and accommodation coordination](/travel-accommodation-assistance), and [interpreter support](/interpreter-language-support) are built into the process — recognizing that surgery itself is often the easiest part of medical travel; the logistics around it are what create the most stress for patients and families.

## Nationality-Specific Guidance

With dedicated [country guides](/countries) for 35+ nations — from [Nigeria](/countries/medical-tourism-india-for-nigerians) and [Kenya](/countries/medical-tourism-india-for-kenyans) to the [UK](/countries/medical-tourism-india-for-uk-patients), [USA](/countries/medical-tourism-india-for-usa-patients), and [Gulf states](/countries/medical-tourism-india-for-uae-residents) — the team understands that a patient traveling from Lagos has different visa, travel, and communication needs than one traveling from London, and plans accordingly.

## A Free Second Opinion, Genuinely Offered

Before any commitment, patients can request a specialist review of their case at no cost — a low-risk way to evaluate both the medical recommendation and the facilitator's responsiveness before deciding to travel. [Request a free second opinion](/free-second-opinion).

How we compare on transparency, network, and support is covered in [Medical Tours India vs other facilitators](/blog/medical-tours-india-vs-other-facilitators).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Medical Tours India trustworthy?**
Judge us the same way you should judge any facilitator: named hospitals, written quotes, second-opinion access, and full-journey support. Those are published on this site and offered before you travel.

**Who operates Medical Tours India?**
Medical Tours India is the patient-facing brand of TechdrHealth, a Hyderabad-based facilitator with MOU partnerships across a 200+ hospital network.

**Do you only work with hospitals in Hyderabad?**
Hyderabad is headquarters, with named partners there and in Delhi. Cases are matched across the wider network after medical review.
