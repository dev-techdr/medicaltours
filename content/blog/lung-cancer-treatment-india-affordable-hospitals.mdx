Lung cancer requires precise staging before any treatment plan. India’s accredited oncology centres offer **thoracic surgery, medical oncology, radiation, and immunotherapy** at substantially lower cost than Western private care — with **multiple hospital quotes** and an optional **video consultation** to validate the pathway before you fly.

## Lung cancer cost comparison (USD, indicative)

| Treatment | India | USA (self-pay) | UK (private) |
|---|---|---|---|
| VATS lobectomy (early stage) | $8,000 – $15,000 | $60,000 – $120,000 | $25,000 – $50,000 |
| Chemotherapy (4–6 cycles) | $3,000 – $12,000 | $40,000 – $100,000 | $15,000 – $40,000 |
| Stereotactic radiation (SBRT) | $5,000 – $12,000 | $50,000 – $90,000 | $22,000 – $45,000 |
| Conventional / IMRT course | $4,000 – $9,000 | $45,000 – $85,000 | $18,000 – $40,000 |
| Immunotherapy (per year, common agents) | $8,000 – $20,000 | $80,000 – $200,000+ | $40,000 – $100,000 |

*Small-cell vs non-small-cell, EGFR/ALK status, and stage drive protocol and price. Common chemo regimens are often $800–$1,100 per daycare session at major Hyderabad centres — confirm per-session vs package.*

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a PET-CT before getting a lung cancer quote?**
Staging scans strongly improve quote accuracy. If you already have PET-CT and biopsy reports, share them. If not, coordinators will advise what investigations to complete first.

**Can immunotherapy for lung cancer be done in India?**
Yes at major centres — confirm the specific agent (e.g., pembrolizumab, nivolumab) and biosimilar vs originator in your written package.

**How long is the hospital stay after lung surgery?**
VATS lobectomy: typically 5–10 days in hospital, 2–3 weeks in India before flying is often advised. Your surgeon confirms based on recovery.

**Can I compare a budget hospital vs a premium option?**
Yes. Shortlists intentionally include different budget tiers when clinically appropriate so you choose based on cost, city, and specialist fit.
