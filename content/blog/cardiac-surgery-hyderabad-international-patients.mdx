Cardiac procedures remain among the most-searched treatments by international patients considering India — and Hyderabad's cardiac care infrastructure has grown substantially to meet that demand.

## Procedure Range

Hyderabad's leading hospitals offer the full spectrum of cardiac care: [coronary bypass (CABG)](/treatments/cardiac-care-india/heart-bypass-surgery-cost-india), [angioplasty and stenting](/treatments/cardiac-care-india/angioplasty-stent-placement-cost-india), valve repair/replacement, pacemaker implantation, and increasingly [TAVR](/treatments/cardiac-care-india/tavr-india) for patients who aren't candidates for open surgery.

Cardiac pathways are coordinated through NABH-accredited partners including [Olive Hospitals](/hospital-network/olive-hospitals), [TX Hospitals](/hospital-network/tx-hospitals), and [Medicover Hospitals](/hospital-network/medicover-hospitals).

## Cost Advantage

A heart bypass procedure in Hyderabad typically costs a fraction of equivalent surgery in the US or UK — while using comparable technology and, in many cases, surgeons trained or fellowship-experienced internationally. Compare numbers in our [CABG cost India vs USA & UK article](/blog/heart-bypass-surgery-cost-india-vs-usa-uk) and the [2026 Hyderabad surgery cost comparison](/blog/hyderabad-surgery-cost-comparison-usa-uk-gulf-2026).

## Why Outcomes Matter More Than Price Alone

Cost savings only matter if outcomes hold up. Reputable Hyderabad cardiac centers publish or can provide success-rate data and are transparent about surgeon experience — ask for this directly rather than relying on marketing claims alone.

## Recovery and Follow-Up

Most cardiac patients require a 2–3 week in-country recovery window before flying home, with hospitals coordinating step-down recovery care and discharge planning specifically built around international patients' travel timelines. [Travel and accommodation assistance](/travel-accommodation-assistance) can cover the recovery stay.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is heart bypass surgery available for international patients in Hyderabad?**
Yes. Accredited Hyderabad hospitals routinely treat overseas patients for CABG, angioplasty, valve procedures, and TAVR.

**How long should I stay in Hyderabad after heart surgery?**
Plan about 2–3 weeks in-country covering evaluation, the procedure, hospital recovery, and a medically advised window before flying.

**How much does CABG cost in Hyderabad vs the US?**
Hyderabad private-hospital pricing is typically a small fraction of US self-pay rates. Always compare written, itemized packages — not headline figures.
