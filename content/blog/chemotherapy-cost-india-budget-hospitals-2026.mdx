Chemotherapy is one of the most common reasons international patients evaluate [cancer treatment in India](/treatments/cancer-treatment-india). The cost gap is real — but the responsible first step is comparing **written chemo packages from multiple budget-friendly hospitals**, not booking on a generic price list.

## Quick chemo cost comparison

| Region | Typical 6-cycle package (USD) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| India | $3,000 – $12,000 | Drug protocol and hospital tier vary |
| USA (self-pay) | $40,000 – $120,000+ | Often itemised per drug + infusion |
| UK (private) | $15,000 – $45,000 | NHS wait times drive private comparison |

*Single-cycle pricing, biosimilar vs originator drugs, and supportive medications change totals significantly.*

## What a chemo package usually includes

- Pre-cycle blood work and fitness checks
- Chemotherapy drugs as quoted (originator or biosimilar — confirm in writing)
- Infusion chair / day-care or inpatient stay as protocol requires
- Nursing, basic supportive meds during infusion
- Oncologist review between cycles

**Usually excluded:** flights, extended hotel stay, home-country follow-up, growth factors or add-on drugs not in the quote.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are Indian chemo drugs the same as in the US or UK?**
Many regimens use the same originator drugs or WHO-approved biosimilars. Always confirm the exact drug names and brands in your written quote.

**How many chemo cycles will I be quoted for?**
Cycle count depends on cancer type, stage, and protocol — typically 4–8 cycles for common solid tumours. Your quote should state the planned number.

**Can I get chemo at a budget hospital and radiation elsewhere?**
Sometimes, but coordinated care at one centre is often simpler. Coordinators can advise after report review.

**How fast can I get chemo package quotes?**
Usually 24–48 hours after complete pathology and staging reports are received.
