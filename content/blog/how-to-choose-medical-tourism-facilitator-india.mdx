Medical tourism to India has grown fast enough that it's attracted both excellent facilitators and opportunistic ones. For a patient researching from abroad, telling the difference isn't always obvious — here's what actually matters.

## Ask for a Written, Itemized Quote — Not a Verbal Estimate

Legitimate facilitators, including [Medical Tours India](/about-us), provide a detailed written package covering procedure cost, hospital stay, and what's excluded, before you commit to anything. Request yours through a [free quote](/get-free-quote). Why this is the main trust signal is covered in [transparent pricing in medical tourism](/blog/transparent-pricing-medical-tourism-trust-signal).

## Check Which Hospitals They Actually Partner With, by Name

Vague claims of "partnerships with top hospitals" without naming them are a red flag. Medical Tours India works directly with named, verifiable partner hospitals — [Olive Hospitals](/hospital-network/olive-hospitals), [TX Hospitals](/hospital-network/tx-hospitals), [Medicover](/hospital-network/medicover-hospitals), and [Max Healthcare Delhi](/hospital-network/max-healthcare-delhi) among them — and will confirm any partnership directly if asked. Browse the [hospital network](/hospital-network).

## Confirm They Don't Charge You Extra on Top of Hospital Pricing

A trustworthy facilitator is transparent about how they're compensated (typically through hospital referral arrangements, not patient markups) — ask directly, and be wary of anyone unwilling to answer.

## Look for a Genuine Free Second-Opinion Offer

Facilitators confident in their hospital network are willing to get you a specialist's honest opinion before you commit to travel — not just a sales pitch dressed as a consultation. See [why a free second opinion before traveling to India matters](/blog/free-second-opinion-before-traveling-india) and [request one](/free-second-opinion).

## Verify They Support the Full Journey, Not Just the Hospital Introduction

[Visa assistance](/medical-visa-assistance), [travel and accommodation](/travel-accommodation-assistance), [interpreter support](/interpreter-language-support), and post-treatment follow-up are what separate a full-service facilitator from someone simply forwarding inquiries to a hospital for a fee. A full breakdown is in [what a medical tourism facilitator actually does](/blog/what-medical-tourism-facilitator-does-full-service).

For scam-specific warning signs, read [how to protect yourself from medical tourism scams in India](/blog/avoid-medical-tourism-scams-india-guide).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I choose a medical tourism company in India?**
Ask for named hospitals, a written itemized quote, a clear compensation model, a free second-opinion pathway, and support that covers visa, travel, language, and follow-up — not only a hospital introduction.

**Should a facilitator charge me on top of hospital prices?**
Ask how they are paid. Reputable facilitators typically work through hospital referral arrangements rather than inflating the clinical bill. Anyone who will not answer that question is a risk.

**Is a verbal cost estimate enough?**
No. You should receive a written package that lists what is included and excluded before you commit.
