Private IVF quotes in Australia and the UK push many couples to compare India. High-volume fertility centres, English-speaking teams, and transparent cycle packages are the usual reasons — not “bargain” marketing.

## How India typically compares

India cycle packages for international patients are often substantially lower than private Australian or UK clinic pricing for comparable protocol classes. Final cost depends on medication, ICSI, genetics screening, and whether embryo freezing or multiple cycles are planned.

Also see [IVF cost India vs UAE/USA](/blog/ivf-treatment-cost-india-vs-uae-usa) and the treatment cost pages via the [cost calculator](/cost-calculator).

## Timing for Australian and British patients

- Confirm protocol and monitoring days before booking long-haul flights
- Plan attendant lodging near the clinic
- Align return travel with early pregnancy scans when success occurs abroad
