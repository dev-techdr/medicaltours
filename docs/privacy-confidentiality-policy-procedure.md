## 1. Purpose

This policy and procedure sets out how TechDr, operating as Medical Tours India, collects, uses, shares, protects, retains, and disposes of patient personal information and medical reports/records while facilitating medical value travel and treatment coordination with partner hospitals and doctors.

It supports confidentiality obligations expected of a medical tourism facilitator and aligns with the organisation’s public Privacy Policy.

## 2. Scope

This document covers:

- Patient personal information
- Medical reports and clinical documents received from patients or authorised representatives
- Enquiry, WhatsApp, email, phone, and form records
- Sharing with partner hospitals and doctors
- Staff access, storage, security, consent, retention, and deletion

It does **not** replace partner hospital medical-record policies. Hospitals remain responsible for their own clinical records after information is shared for treatment.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Personal information | Identifiable patient details (name, contact, nationality, passport/visa support data, etc.) |
| Medical information | History, diagnosis, labs, imaging, prescriptions, discharge notes, and similar clinical documents |
| Authorised staff | Team members with a job-related need to access a patient file |
| Partner hospital/doctor | Facility or clinician engaged for opinion, admission, or treatment coordination |
| Consent | Clear permission from the patient or authorised representative to collect, use, or share information for facilitation |

## 4. Policy statements

### 4.1 Confidentiality

1. All patient personal and medical information is confidential.
2. Staff must not discuss, forward, screenshot, or disclose patient details except for authorised care coordination.
3. Access is limited to authorised staff on a need-to-know basis.
4. Patient cases must not be used in marketing, social media, or testimonials without documented consent.

### 4.2 Collection

We collect only information reasonably required to:

- Respond to enquiries
- Obtain hospital/doctor opinions
- Prepare cost and treatment guidance
- Support visa, travel, and logistics coordination
- Communicate with the patient and nominated contacts

Sources include phone, WhatsApp, email, website forms, and documents voluntarily shared by the patient or authorised representative.

### 4.3 Use

Information may be used only for facilitation purposes described above and for legitimate operational, legal, or quality needs. Personal data is not sold.

### 4.4 Sharing with hospitals and doctors

1. Relevant medical and contact details may be shared with partner hospitals/doctors **with patient consent** (or consent of an authorised representative), for treatment evaluation and coordination only.
2. Share only what is necessary for the requested opinion or pathway.
3. Prefer secure or agreed channels used by the partner hospital’s international patient desk.
4. Record when and to whom key clinical packages were shared (hospital name, date, channel).

### 4.5 Other sharing

Limited technical/advertising analytics data may be processed via website tools (for example Meta or Google) as described in the public Privacy Policy. **Medical reports must not be uploaded to advertising platforms.**

Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law.

### 4.6 Patient consent

1. Submitting an enquiry and medical documents is treated as consent to collect and use information for facilitation.
2. Hospital/doctor sharing is done with consent or at the patient’s request.
3. Patients may withdraw consent for further facilitation by contacting the privacy contact above.
4. Withdrawal does not reverse information already lawfully shared with a hospital at the patient’s request.

### 4.7 Data security

Staff must:

- Use organisation-approved accounts/devices where provided
- Protect passwords and lock devices when unattended
- Avoid unnecessary local downloads of medical files
- Not forward patient files to personal email or personal cloud storage
- Use group chats carefully; limit membership to people involved in the case
- Report suspected loss, unauthorised access, or disclosure immediately to the Policy Owner

Reasonable organisational measures include restricted staff access, password-protected systems, and careful handling of documents received by email or messaging apps.

### 4.8 Retention and deletion

1. Retain enquiry and coordination records as long as reasonably needed for service continuity, operational/legal requirements, and dispute handling.
2. As a general rule, inactive enquiry files may be reviewed for deletion or anonymisation after up to **seven (7) years**, unless a longer period is required by law, ongoing coordination, or a documented legitimate need.
3. Patients may request access, correction, or deletion via the privacy contact. Requests should be acknowledged and actioned within a reasonable time (target: **15 working days**), subject to legal and operational limits.

## 5. Procedures

### 5.1 Receiving a new patient enquiry

1. Create or update the patient file with identity and contact details.
2. Confirm preferred communication channel and any attendant/family contact authorised to receive updates.
3. Request only medical documents needed for the current enquiry.
4. Store documents in the designated case folder/system; avoid duplicate uncontrolled copies.

### 5.2 Handling medical reports

1. Label files clearly with patient name and date received.
2. Do not alter clinical content.
3. Share with hospitals/doctors only after consent/request is clear.
4. When sharing, include necessary demographics and the clinical package; omit unrelated personal notes.

### 5.3 Obtaining and recording consent for hospital sharing

Before sending medical information to a hospital or doctor:

1. Confirm the patient (or authorised representative) has asked for an opinion/coordination, or has agreed to sharing.
2. Note consent in the case record (date, channel — e.g. WhatsApp/email/call — and what will be shared).
3. Send to the appropriate hospital international desk / coordinator / doctor channel.
4. Log destination hospital and date sent.

### 5.4 Staff access control

1. Only staff assigned to the case (or covering duty) may access the full file.
2. New joiners receive briefing on this policy before handling patient documents.
3. Departing staff must return devices/access and must not retain patient files.

### 5.5 Patient access, correction, or deletion request

1. Verify the requester’s identity and authority.
2. Locate relevant records held by TechDr / Medical Tours India.
3. Provide a copy or summary of personal information held, or correct inaccurate details, where feasible.
4. For deletion: remove or anonymise records under our control where retention is no longer required; explain if some data must be retained (legal hold, completed coordination trail, or already shared with a hospital).
5. Record the request and outcome in the case notes.

### 5.6 Suspected breach or loss of information

1. Immediately notify the Policy Owner.
2. Contain the issue (revoke access, recover device, stop further forwarding).
3. Document what happened, what data was involved, and who was affected.
4. Assess whether the patient and/or partner hospital should be informed.
5. Implement corrective action (process change, access review, staff reminder).

- Confirmed acknowledgment may be recorded by the Operations Lead.

## 8. Compliance and discipline

Failure to follow this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of engagement, and may be reported where legally required.

## 9. Review

This policy and procedure is reviewed at least annually, or sooner after a material incident, legal change, or process change. The public Privacy Policy should be updated when practices described here change in a way that affects patients.
